
Interview Answer

Agar interviewer puche:

Are there types of Encapsulation?

Short answer:

Yes. Commonly Encapsulation is discussed as:

Data Encapsulation → Hiding and protecting data.
Method Encapsulation → Hiding internal implementation methods.
Class Encapsulation → Bundling data and behavior into a single unit.

And in practice, Data Encapsulation is the most important and most commonly referred type when people talk about Encapsulation in OOP.
